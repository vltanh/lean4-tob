# Tree of blobs inference from quartets, in Lean

A Lean 4 formalization of three papers on inferring the tree of blobs of a phylogenetic network from gene trees under the
network multispecies coalescent: **ECToBlob** (Rhodes, Allman, Ané, Baños 2026), **TOB-QMC** (Dai, Han, Molloy 2026) and **TINNiK**
(Allman, Baños, Mitchell, Rhodes 2024). The tree of blobs is what remains of a network when its reticulate parts are
contracted. These papers infer it from 4-taxon concordance factors, and the main results say that it is
recovered with probability tending to one as the number of gene trees grows.

The formalization is unconditional: no result of the literature is assumed, including the CF-detectability of 4-blobs for generic parameters (Allman et al. 2023, Corollary 1), which is proved here.

## What is proved

- Every numbered result of the three papers, by the paper's argument except for the departures listed below. [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) has the
  list, with what each depends on (Section 8).
- The results the papers cite: Allman et al. 2023 (Theorems 1, 2, Corollary 1, Lemmas 2, 3), Rhodes 2020 (Theorem 2), Huber et al. 2025 (Theorem 5.1, forward), Ané et al. 2024 (exchangeability), Mitchell et al. 2019 (Theorem 3.1, for this model), NANUQ 2019 (Proposition 9; Theorem 36, corrected). They are in [`External/`](External).
- What the papers say in passing, where it's mathematics and true: ECToBlob's Remarks 3.3, 5.2, 8.3, and Remark 8.4 for binary networks; TOB-QMC's Lemma 1, supplement Lemma 8, and its bipartition search with 3-fix-1-alter; the T3 option of TINNiK and TOB-QMC; neighbor joining in TINNiK's Step 4, for every tie-break; ASTRAL's dynamic program on its search space; counts of operations behind the running-time claims; and from ECToBlob's §10, the line test without the no-anomaly assumption, a random `T₀`, data splitting, and combining edge p-values. [REPORT.md, Section 9](REPORT.md#9-not-formalized) lists what remains.
- The objects themselves: rooted networks as multigraphs; cut edges; the tree of blobs and the induced networks as graphs, with bridges to the split
  families the statements use; Kingman's coalescent; the network multispecies coalescent on arbitrary networks with correlated inheritance, as a
  probability law on binary trees; the star, T1 and cut tests, and their limit laws from the central limit theorem.
- `lake build` passes with no warnings other than the `sorry`s of the statements in [`Challenge.lean`](Challenge.lean), which are there by design. There is no `axiom`, `native_decide` or `implemented_by`.
  [`scripts/Audit.lean`](scripts/Audit.lean) checks every declaration of the libraries, including private and generated ones, against [`propext`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Init/Core.html#propext), [`Classical.choice`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Init/Prelude.html#Classical.choice), [`Quot.sound`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Init/Prelude.html#Quot.sound).

## The main results

Each is stated in [`Challenge.lean`](Challenge.lean), and proved in [`Solution.lean`](Solution.lean) from the library.

- [`Challenge.theorem3_1`](Challenge.lean#L848) (ECToBlob 3.1): every tree maximizing the Maximum Quartet Support objective resolves the tree of blobs, for quartet probabilities with strict dominance across cut edges and exchangeability across them.
- [`Challenge.theorem4_1`](Challenge.lean#L859) (ECToBlob 4.1): if `a₁a₂|b₁b₂` is a cut-edge split of every induced 4-taxon network for `aᵢ ∈ Aᵢ`, `bᵢ ∈ Bᵢ`, then `A₁ ∪ A₂ | B₁ ∪ B₂` is a cut-edge split of the network.
- [`Challenge.theorem8_2`](Challenge.lean#L879) (ECToBlob 8.2, corrected): there are test levels `α_n, β_n`, independent of the network, such that every variant of the ECToBlob algorithm, from any resolution of the tree of blobs, returns the tree of blobs with probability tending to one.
- [`Challenge.theorem1`](Challenge.lean#L899), [`Challenge.theorem1_optimal`](Challenge.lean#L912) (TOB-QMC 1): optimal solutions of Weighted Quartet Consensus refine the tree of blobs, almost surely for all large numbers of gene trees.
- [`Challenge.theorem2`](Challenge.lean#L927) (TOB-QMC 2): on a binary level-1 network, an oracle for treelikeness on a disjoint cover around each edge of a refinement finds the edges to contract.
- [`Challenge.consistency`](Challenge.lean#L939) (TOB-QMC §4.3): the resulting method is consistent for level-1 networks, under the paper's assumptions.
- [`Challenge.theorem4`](Challenge.lean#L959) (TINNiK 4): for generic parameters there are levels `α_m → 0` such that the TINNiK algorithm is consistent, for any `β > 0` and exact tree builder.

## Audit summary

The numbers refer to [REPORT.md](REPORT.md), which gives the details and the repairs.

- **Errors.** ECToBlob's (3.3) overcounts (E2). Its Remark 8.4 is false for non-binary networks, by an explicit tree and `T₀` (E35), and true for binary ones. Its Remark 3.3 needs "no anomalous quartets" read strictly: under the cited definition, (3.1) and the conclusion of Theorem 3.1 fail at a `3₂`-cycle (E36). TOB-QMC's supplement: Lemma 7 and Theorem 1 are false at level 2, by an explicit network (E25); Lemma 2's converse is false (E23); positive lengths don't exclude the centroid (E28). TINNiK: the count `4(N-1)` should be `4(N-4)` (E29), and the MLE need not exist on the open cut model (E31). Among results only cited: NANUQ's Theorem 36 is false as printed, at the distance, and Studier and Keppler's Lemma fails at polytomies.
- **Gaps.** All are repaired, and only E22, which makes supplement Lemma 4 vacuous as printed, changes a statement. The largest: in the proof of ECToBlob's Theorem 8.2, the witness of a false edge may have a trivial 4-blob, where the T1 null holds, which needed a new argument (E12). Others in that proof: E13–E17. Elsewhere: E1, E3, E4, E6, E7, E37, E38; TOB-QMC E18–E22, E24, E39; TINNiK E30, E32, E33, E40.
- **Missing hypotheses.** ECToBlob's Theorem 8.2 needs parameters meeting Assumption 1 (E10, agreed with the user), and, for its Cauchy and CBC variants, positive alternatives on null quartets, with a counterexample at a 5-sunlet under the continuous extension of the Cauchy transform at `p = 1` (E11, agreed with the user). The latter does not affect the NMSC.
- **Redundant hypotheses.** In ECToBlob's Theorem 4.1, `A₁, A₂` and `B₁, B₂` needn't be disjoint. In TOB-QMC's Lemma 4, of the hypotheses on the refinement only [`RefinesBlob`](TOBQMC/Level1.lean#L46) matters, and Corollary 1 doesn't need `B` binary. In TINNiK's Theorem 4, `0 ≤ δ` isn't used.
- **Use of cited results.** TINNiK's use of its Theorem 2 needs a stronger rule than hypothesis (b) (E30, proved). TOB-QMC cites TINNiK's Theorem 4 for a step of its proof, which is stated for other parameters; the claim holds under TOB-QMC's own assumptions (E21). ECToBlob's adaptation of Allman et al.'s Theorem 1, by "checking the argument", turns out right (E5).
- **Proofs.** They follow the sources' arguments, cited results included. The route check confirms that each uses the results its paper's proof cites, except five recorded differences. Every remaining departure is in [REPORT.md, Section 7](REPORT.md#7-departures-from-the-papers-proofs), with its reason. Gaps repaired: ECToBlob's Theorems 3.1 and 8.2 and Corollary 3.6, TOB-QMC's Lemma 4, Corollary 1 and supplement Lemma 4, TINNiK's Theorem 4 under the alternative. Mathematics Lean lacks: TINNiK's Proposition 5, where Mitchell et al.'s Theorem 3.1 is proved directly for this model. Not forced, and documented: in ECToBlob's Theorem 4.1 and Proposition 5.1, TOB-QMC's Lemmas 1 and 4 and supplement Lemma 8, and Allman et al.'s Theorem 1 (no 2-blob step, no Lemma 1, one argument across the rooted forms, deleted networks in the coordinates of `N`) and Corollary 1 (the induced network cut at the LSA). Shorter arguments that were replaced are kept in [REPORT.md, Section 11](REPORT.md#11-whats-next).

## Related work

From [REPORT.md, Section 10](REPORT.md#10-the-papers-in-the-literature).

- No formalization of these papers was found other than this one. The closest is the public [lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics), whose Kingman coalescent modules this project adapts; its level-1 network coalescent isn't used.
- History: Allman et al. 2023 showed tree of blobs identifiability from quartets, with no bound on the level; TINNiK made it an algorithm with a consistency theorem. Huber et al. 2025 and Frohn et al. 2025 give the exact-quarnet form that ECToBlob's Theorem 4.1 extends. ECToBlob's Corollary 4.2 is, for binary networks, Lemma 1 of Frohn et al., as ECToBlob says.
- Concurrent: TOB-QMC and ECToBlob prove the same result that an optimum of the quartet criterion refines the tree of blobs, and ECToBlob calls the two works independent.
- Since: Dai and Molloy (WABI 2026) reconstruct level-1 blobs from a tree of blobs, using TOB-QMC's Theorem 2. We found no erratum or published counterexample to the three papers; the errors recorded here are this audit's own.

## What's next

The natural next steps are to extend the generic results beyond `ρ = 0`, and to settle whether treelikeness of the quartets around an edge decides its membership in the tree of blobs beyond level 1, which ECToBlob's §10 raises. [REPORT.md, Section 11](REPORT.md#11-whats-next) has these and the simpler arguments that the formalization found, among them the funnel step of ECToBlob's Theorem 4.1, by domination.

## Building

```sh
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake env lean -DautoImplicit=false -DmaxSynthPendingDepth=3 scripts/Audit.lean   # axioms and dependencies
python3 scripts/route_check.py check docs/paper_routes.tsv --accept docs/route_differences.tsv
lake env lake comparator --config=comparator.json
```

Toolchain: Lean and Mathlib `v4.36.0-rc1` ([`lean-toolchain`](lean-toolchain), [`lake-manifest.json`](lake-manifest.json)). After changing the shared definitions, run `python3 scripts/sync_challenge_defs.py`; after changing a statement in the Challenge, `python3 scripts/gen_solution.py`; after adding a module, `python3 scripts/gen_audit.py`; and after moving declarations, `python3 scripts/linkify_docs.py` to update the documents' links. `scripts/*Check.lean` check by themselves some counterexamples of the audit. [`docs/CONFIGURATION.md`](docs/CONFIGURATION.md) records the versions and decisions; [`docs/ADAPTED_SOURCES.md`](docs/ADAPTED_SOURCES.md) and [`docs/COALESCENT_SOURCES.md`](docs/COALESCENT_SOURCES.md) record adapted code.
