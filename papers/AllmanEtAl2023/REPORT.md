# Audit of Allman et al. 2023 and its formalization

Paper: E. S. Allman, H. Baños, J. D. Mitchell, J. A. Rhodes, *The tree of blobs of a species network: identifiability under the coalescent*, [J. Math. Biol. 86 (2023), 10](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-022-01838-9), read in the PMC author manuscript NIHMS1884071 ([PMC10062380](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10062380/)) and the [arXiv TeX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03466) of 2205.03466v1, its only version. The audit was made against both. The journal's text wasn't reachable, so the findings are about the author manuscript, and Section 3 marks those that are in the manuscript only. "Line N" is a line of the plain text of the PMC page, one line per paragraph, as [`docs/CONFIGURATION.md`](../../docs/CONFIGURATION.md) defines it ("the PMC text, line N"). The `a:` labels of the route check are the arXiv TeX's for numbered results, and this project's otherwise. Section, result and equation numbers are the paper's.

This paper is one of four in the collection described in [`README.md`](../../README.md). Three others there, TINNiK, TOB-QMC and ECToBlob, build on it (Section 2). The findings are numbered across the collection: here E55–E66, and E81, on Baños 2019, which the paper cites.

They came from reading and proving the paper, and are reported for the user who requested this formalization to read. ## 1. Summary

- **Errors.** No numbered result of the paper fails, reading a subnetwork as connected; read literally, Lemma 2 does (E55). A side remark in the proof of Proposition 1 is false for some `ε` (E57). The rest are smaller points: misprints, some in the PMC manuscript only (E57, E63, E66), and a loose sentence on the distance between blobs (E65).
- **Gaps.** E56, E58–E62. All are repaired, and none changes a statement.
- **Missing hypotheses.** Lemma 2 needs `M` connected (E55). The remark at line 252 that edge lengths between trivial blobs are identifiable holds for internal edges, a qualification it doesn't state (E64). Section 4 has both.
- **Redundant hypotheses.** Section 5. The most notable: the binary hypothesis in Theorem 2 and Lemma 3, and in the combinatorial part of Theorem 3.
- **Use of cited results.** Correct: Baños 2019, after Proposition 1, for the values that arise, and Semple and Steel 2005 with Rhodes 2020, in Theorem 4. The proof of Proposition 7 of Baños 2019 has a misprint (E81). Of the later papers' uses of this one, TINNiK applies Theorem 2 beyond its printed hypothesis (b) (TINNiK's E30), and ECToBlob's Proposition 5.1 adapts Theorem 1 by "checking the argument", which turns out right (ECToBlob's E5). Section 2 has these.

## 3. Errors and gaps in the paper

"Line N" here is a line of the plain text of the PMC page of the author manuscript NIHMS1884071, as in [`docs/CONFIGURATION.md`](../../docs/CONFIGURATION.md) ("the PMC text, line N"). This manuscript and the arXiv TeX were both read, and the journal's text wasn't reachable, so nothing here was checked against it. The items in the manuscript only are marked; the rest are in the TeX as well. The later papers cite this one, and none of their uses is affected (Section 2). No numbered result of the paper fails, reading a subnetwork as connected; read literally, Lemma 2 does (E55). E57 and E64 record sentences that fail as read literally, and the rest are gaps, loose sentences and misprints.

**E55. Lemma 2 (a missing word; the statement needs it).** Line 184: "Consider a network N with degree-1 nodes bijectively labelled by X, and a subnetwork M of N with the restricted labelling of some degree-1 nodes by Y ⊆ X. If a set S ⊆ Y determines a blob on M, then S determines a blob on N." Lemma 2 needs `M` connected, or at least each `s` joined to `ℬ₀` in `M`. Its proof assumes this and all its uses satisfy it, but the word is missing. For disconnected `M`, Definition 8 can hold vacuously and the conclusion fails. No result of the paper is affected. The paper writes "connected subnetwork" where it means that (line 207), so the literal reading is available. The counterexample is the caterpillar `(((a, b), c), d)` with `M` the pendant edges: [`AllmanEtAl2023.lemma2_needs_connected`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Lemma2Counterexample.lean#L163). [`AllmanEtAl2023.lemma2`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Lemma2.lean#L146) takes `M` connected, which is what its uses have ([`AllmanEtAl2023.isConnectedSubnetwork_ancestralArcs`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Corollary2.lean#L43)).

**E56. Lemma 2: the degree-one hypothesis, and blobs of three taxa (gaps).** "With degree-1 nodes bijectively labelled by X" is more than the uses satisfy: at line 215 Lemma 3 applies Lemma 2 with `M` in the role of `N`, and the root of `M` can have degree 1 there, with no label. The proof doesn't use it: [`AllmanEtAl2023.IsLabelledNetwork`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Lemma2.lean#L30) asks for distinct labels on nodes of degree 1. In the proof, "it has a subpath from ℬ to s entirely outside of ℬ" (line 185) needs `s ∉ ℬ`, which holds since a degree-one node is alone in its blob, and such a blob isn't determined by three or more labels. And line 73, "on a network every subset of 3 taxa determines a blob", has no proof: [`AllmanEtAl2023.BlobStructure.existsUnique_isDeterminedBy_three`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobStructure.lean#L305), through [`AllmanEtAl2023.exists_median`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/SubgraphBlobs.lean#L588).

**E57. Proposition 1 (four small points; the proposition holds as printed).**

- **Line 165: the lengths (a misprint, manuscript only).** "the pendant edges to a, b, c, d to be of lengths 1, 1, 1 + (k + 1)ϵ + (2^k − 1)M, respectively" gives three lengths for four edges. The TeX has `1, 1, 1 + (k+1)ε, 2 + (k+1)ε + (2^k − 1)M`, and Figure 6 agrees. Lean uses those ([`AllmanEtAl2023.NetworkNk.metricNk`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/NetworkNk.lean#L135)), which make the network ultrametric as the sentence says ([`AllmanEtAl2023.NetworkNk.heightNk_source`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/NetworkNkGraph.lean#L791)).
- **Line 166: "Note that by Theorem 1, CF_abcd is strictly (ab|cd)-cut for ϵ > 0" (false as a claim on all `ε > 0`; unused).** Theorem 1 is generic, and the proposition's own intermediate-value argument reaches `(1/3, 1/3, 1/3)`: [`AllmanEtAl2023.NetworkNk.exists_not_isStrictlyCutAtFirst`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Proposition1.lean#L229). No step uses the sentence.
- **Lines 163, 167, 173: `p` means two things (notation).** It is an entry of the triple in the statement, and `exp(-ϵ)` in the proof; the last paragraph writes `(q, p, p)`, `q + 2p = 1`, for the statement's `(p, q, q)`.
- **Line 164: "By statement 1 of Theorem 1" (a loose citation that holds).** That statement is for generic parameters, and the proposition is for all valid ones. Generic parameters are dense and CFs are continuous, so the citation is good: [`AllmanEtAl2023.ForGenericParams.eq_zero_of_continuous`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/GenericDense.lean#L24), [`AllmanEtAl2023.proposition1`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Proposition1.lean#L62).

**E58. Theorem 1, statement (b) (gaps; the result holds).**

- **Lines 147–148: "suppressing any degree-2 nodes" isn't enough.** A root can lose a child, and then it has out-degree 1, which Definition 1 excludes. On the 4-cycle with arcs `r→v, r→x, x→b, x→y, y→c, y→z, z→d, z→v, v→a`, deleting `r→v` does this. Step 3 of Definition 6 is what's needed, and the CFs are unaffected ([`AllmanEtAl2023.nmscCF_removeStem`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/InducedCF.lean#L470)).
- **Line 149: "Now e must be a cut edge in both N1 and N2" has no reason.** It's true: a cut edge of `Nᵢ` with split `ab|cd` lies in `N₃` between `ℬ` and `b`, so the attachments of `a` lie beyond it, and hence beyond `e` ([`AllmanEtAl2023.IsLowPair.ends_mem_far`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Reduction.lean#L277), [`AllmanEtAl2023.IsLowPair.onlyCrosses_far_insert`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Reduction.lean#L337)).
- **Line 146: "Doing so, we have effectively removed these blobs"** has no reason ([`AllmanEtAl2023.nmscCF_contracted`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ContractBlob.lean#L724), [`AllmanEtAl2023.exists_isContractible_of_cutDegree_two`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ContractBlob.lean#L1080)). **Line 148: "since 𝒩− has no 3-blobs"** leaves unproved the 3-blob at the parent of a cherry ([`AllmanEtAl2023.exists_cutDegree_three_of_cherry`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem1b.lean#L289)).
- **Implicit:** that a lowest hybrid node exists, and how generic parameters on `𝒩⁺` reach the `𝒩ᵢ⁺` at line 152, which states the choice with no reason ([`AllmanEtAl2023.measure_deleted_preimage`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem1b.lean#L82)).

**E59. Corollary 1 (a gap; the result holds).** Line 157, "they give rise to a generic condition on numerical parameter values on the n-taxon network", needs that the map of parameters to an induced network sends valid parameters to valid ones, and that preimages of null sets are null: [`AllmanEtAl2023.isMetric_inducedParams`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ClaimsTheorem1.lean#L52), [`AllmanEtAl2023.isGenericNull_inducedParams`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ClaimsTheorem1.lean#L73). It also needs the identity of the CFs, which line 181 states "by the structure of the NMSC model": [`AllmanEtAl2023.nmscCF_induced`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ClaimsTheorem1.lean#L36).

**E60. Corollary 2 and Lemma 3: the stem (a gap, manuscript only).** Line 186 says that induced networks "are obtained from subnetworks by suppressing degree-2 nodes", and line 207 likewise. The manuscript's Definition 6 has a third step that removes a stem from the root, and neither passage mentions it. (The TeX's Definition 6 has only the first two steps.) Blobs and cut edges pass through it: [`AllmanEtAl2023.Bridges.isBlobQuartetOnInduced_iff`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Bridges.lean#L636), [`AllmanEtAl2023.Bridges.induced_hasCutSplit_iff`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Bridges.lean#L210).

**E61. Lemma 3 (omitted steps; the result holds).**

- Line 208: "Thus e0 also separates a, b, from c, d" has no reason: the side containing `c, d` avoids `ℬ′` and is connected.
- Line 213: "Thus there is a path P3 from e0 to z in K_cd": the reason gives connectivity in `N′` only, and the path has to be kept in `K_cd`.
- Line 214: "This cycle also lies in M, as none of its edges are ancestral only to the taxon d. ## 6. How the formalization reads the paper

- **The paper's objects.** The results as printed are on [`AllmanEtAl2023.IsBinaryNetwork`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Definitions.lean#L61), its Definition 1 verbatim, tied to the library's definitions by [`AllmanEtAl2023.isBinaryNetwork_iff`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Definitions.lean#L139). [`AllmanEtAl2023.Trd`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobDefinitions.lean#L422) is `T_rd(𝒩⁻)` as a labelled graph, and [`AllmanEtAl2023.Bridges.splits_Trd`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Bridges.lean#L353) says its splits are the library's [`Phylogenetics.treeOfBlobs`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Network/Quartets.lean#L40). The topologies of Figure 4 are listed literally by `HasQuartetTopology` and `HasStarTopology` on [`AllmanEtAl2023.LabelledGraph`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobDefinitions.lean#L385).
- **Networks and the coalescent** are the library's. Networks are directed multigraphs with named arcs ([`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Network/Defs.lean#L30)), rooted, with parallel arcs allowed. The NMSC on arbitrary networks is an order-free sum over histories ([`Phylogenetics.geneTreeDist`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Defs.lean#L94)), the product over vertices and arcs of the joint law of the forests at the bottom and top of each arc, with one lineage per taxon. Its parameter `ρ` is the inheritance correlation; the paper's model is `ρ = 0`, and the theorems are stated for `nmscCF N θ 0`.
- **The manuscript and the TeX.** Definition 6 follows the manuscript, with its third step that removes a stem ([`AllmanEtAl2023.induced`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/InducedNetwork.lean#L296)). The lengths of `𝒩(k)⁺` follow the TeX and Figure 6, and the manuscript's lengths are a misprint (E57). The two versions also differ in Definition 8's wording, with the same meaning; the manuscript's fuller proof of Theorem 1 (b), with `w`, `k₁`, `k₂`, Figure 5 and "only a single edge below v"; its misprints in the proof of Lemma 3 (E66); and more detail on the algorithm in Section 5. A comparison word by word found nothing else mathematical.
- **"Identifiable from `CF_Q`" (Corollary 1).** [`AllmanEtAl2023.readTopology`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L293), a fixed function of the triple, returns the topology of `T_rd(𝒩⁻_Q)` for generic parameters, with one exceptional set per network for all `Q`.
- **"Can be identified" (Theorem 3).** [`AllmanEtAl2023.CFInferred`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L319) is a predicate on the CFs alone: what [`readTopology`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L293) reads as unresolved, closed under the printed rule, whose third hypothesis is read off the CF of the third set. [`AllmanEtAl2023.theorem3`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem3.lean#L510) says that it holds exactly on the B-quartets, for generic parameters; the direction from [`CFInferred`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L319) to B-quartet, which "identified" needs, is Theorem 2.
- **"Identifiable" (Theorem 4).** There is one function `F` of the CFs, chosen before the network, with `F(CF)` the splits of `T_rd(𝒩⁻)` off a null set of each network's parameters ([`AllmanEtAl2023.theorem4`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem4.lean#L103)). ### Before the paper

The paper's own account: NANUQ, Baños 2019 and Solís-Lemus, Ané 2016 on level 1; identifiability from induced 4-taxon networks (Huber et al. 2018); and that the tree of blobs comes from Gusfield et al. 2007, with "a closely related notion" in Murakami et al., [Bull. Math. Biol. 81 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-019-00641-w).

- Gusfield, Bansal, Bafna, Song, [J. Comput. Biol. 14 (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1089/cmb.2006.0137): a decomposition theorem for phylogenetic networks that exposes "a maximal embedded tree structure". It is formalized separately, in [lean4-gusfield2007](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-gusfield2007).
- Solís-Lemus, Ané, [PLoS Genet. 12 (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1005896) (SNaQ, pseudolikelihood on quartets); Baños, [Bull. Math. Biol. 81 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-018-0485-4) ([arXiv 2017-11-28](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10545); read for its Proposition 7): "every cycle of size at least 4 and every hybrid node in a cycle of size at least 5" of a level-1 network are identifiable from gene tree quartets; Allman, Baños, Rhodes, [NANUQ, Algorithms Mol. Biol. 14 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13015-019-0159-2) ([arXiv 2019-05-16](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07050)): level-1 inference from quartet hypothesis tests and a quartet intertaxon distance.
- Xu, Ané, [J. Math. Biol. 86 (2023), 12](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-022-01847-8) ([arXiv 2021-10-22](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.11814)): the "distance split tree" built from average pairwise distances refines the tree of blobs, and at level 1 equals the tree of blobs refined to separate polytomies from blobs (Theorem 10). The paper cites the preprint, as Xu and Ané 2021, for definitions (Section 2).

Section 2 has the cited results that the proofs use.

What the paper adds: "most features of the unrooted tree of blobs" are determined by the distribution of gene quartet topologies, with no assumption on the level, through a B-quartet inference rule. It names the difficulty that the later work meets in different ways: a B-quartet of the network can have a cut-edge split on its induced 4-taxon network, "the main obstacle". It works at any level and uses resolved topologies with the inference rule, since "the induced network on a B-quartet may or may not have a 4-blob, and can even be a tree".

## 11. What's next

### Open directions

- **Correlated inheritance.** The paper's model has independent inheritance, `ρ = 0`. For binary networks at generic parameters, "Beyond level-1" (Proposition 5.1, Section 10) extends the arguments of this paper to common inheritance, `ρ = 1`, for tree of blobs identifiability, and leaves other `ρ` aside. ECToBlob's §5 says "We strongly suspect Assumption 1 also holds for the NMSC model with common or correlated inheritance". So what is open is `0 < ρ < 1`, and `ρ = 1` on non-binary networks with ECToBlob's measure. [`AllmanEtAl2023.cfDetectable_generic`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/CFDetectable.lean#L24) here covers only `ρ = 0`. [`Phylogenetics.geneTreeDist`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Defs.lean#L94) includes `ρ`, and the equalities of the forward clauses of ECToBlob's Assumption 1 are proved for all `ρ` ([`Phylogenetics.NMSC.cut_alternatives`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Exchangeability.lean#L550), [`Phylogenetics.NMSC.trivial_uniform`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Exchangeability.lean#L771)). What is needed is the generic clause: a nonvanishing witness for each 4-taxon network, for each `ρ`.
- **Distances between blobs above level 1.** Line 252 leaves this to future work, and level 1 is settled in Allman et al. 2024 (Section 10). Above it: which cut edges have identifiable lengths, and which functions of the lengths are identifiable? [`AllmanEtAl2023.InPassing.blobDistance_not_identifiable`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobDistance.lean#L115) is the 4-taxon case.
- **`T̄` and the tree of blobs.** A remark that is unverified: on paper, not in Lean, and read by no independent checker. For a binary network, `T̄` of all the splits of its displayed trees should be its tree of blobs, at any level. A split `A|B` of `T̄` is compatible with every displayed split, so every displayed tree shows `a₁a₂|b₁b₂` across it, so by [`AllmanEtAl2023.InPassing.isTreelike_iff_forall_displays`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/InPassing.lean#L513) and [`ECToBlob.theorem4_1`](../../src/ECToBlob/Section4.lean#L55) (or Huber et al.'s Theorem 5.1) it is a cut-edge split. Conversely a cut-edge split is in every displayed tree and is a trivial component. A script tried it on 3,000 random binary networks with at most four reticulations; the script isn't kept in the repository. Under the displayed tree model `T̄` would then be an exact estimator. Under the coalescent, where every pair of gene-tree splits is eventually incompatible, is anything left of this?

### Simpler proofs

We found none of these published, in the search of Section 10.

- **Theorem 1, part (b).** Instead of one parameter with three distinct CFs, take each pair of quartet trees separately. The reduction shows that the display trees of a non-treelike 4-set show at least two different quartets, so at a display tree with long arcs one of the pair has CF near 1. Non-cut then holds generically with no mixture in `γ`. This is a sketch; the formal proof follows the source's mixture.
- **Theorem 1, the choice of `γ`.** One explicit point suffices: all lengths 3 and `γ = 1/3`. Each endpoint CF is within `exp(-3) < 1/4` of a vertex, so the mixture has entries near `1/3`, `2/3`, `0`, with no genericity at all. An earlier version of the formal proof did this; it now follows the source.
- **Theorem 1, the contradiction.** With no blobs: `e₁` and `e₂` dominate a common taxon, so they are ordered, and in four cases by direction the arc nearer the blob has `a` on the `b` side in both deletions. An earlier version of the formal proof did this.
- **Theorem 1 (b), the 2-blob step and Lemma 1.** Neither is needed: a reticulation in a 2-blob passes the same deletion test as any other, since domination sees through 2-blobs, and "a single edge below `v`" follows from the `2|2` split against an unresolved `T`. Checked in Lean, in [`AllmanEtAl2023.exists_reduction_aux`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Reduction.lean#L673), which the later papers use; the numbered result [`AllmanEtAl2023.theorem1_b`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem1b.lean#L716) follows the paper.
- **Corollary 1.** On `N` directly: statements (a) and (b) hold for a 4-set of any network, since domination off `D` sees the four taxa alone and CFs are analytic in `N`'s coordinates. This needs neither the induced network, nor the map of parameters, nor the identity of CFs. An earlier version of the formal proof did this (in the repository's history); the formal proof now follows the source, through [`AllmanEtAl2023.induced`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/InducedNetwork.lean#L296).
- **Proposition 1, the bound on `CF_{ab|cd}`.** The bound alone has a shorter proof, by a potential on the event that the lineages diverge, with no conditional masses or species trees. An earlier version of the formal proof did this (in the repository's history); the formal proof now follows the paper.
- **Blobs through suppression.** "Determines" is equivalent to "no side of a cut edge away from the blob has two of the taxa" ([`AllmanEtAl2023.BlobsPass.determines_iff_noTwoInSide`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobsPass.lean#L38)), which makes the passage of blobs through Definition 6 short. Checked in Lean.
- **Theorem 2.** Take a side `A` of the tree of blobs with two taxa of the set: the two B-quartets constrain `A` on `a, b, c, d, e`, and 32 cases leave only `A` separating `a, e` from `b, c, d`, which is the contradiction. No node of the tree of blobs is needed. An earlier version of the formal proof did this.
- **Lemma 3, in the language of domination.** Since `e₀` is a cut on `Y \ {α}` and not on `Y`, the taxon `α` is below `e₀` and not dominated by it, and an arc resolving `{a,b,c,α}` on `Y \ {d}` compares with `e₀` by [`AllmanEtAl2023.order_of_isBelow_of_dominates`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/CutOrder.lean#L61), in four cases. Components and the cycle through `A, K_ab, e₀, K_cd` do not appear. It takes about 126 lines against about 1,600 for the source's route. An earlier version of the formal proof did this (in the repository's history); the formal proof now follows the source. Smaller changes also remain possible within the source's route: `C` as a minimal subset of all of `P₁ ∪ P₂ ∪ P₃ ∪ P₄` closing up `e₀` must leave `K_ab` at `x`, so `P₁` itself is the path from `α` to `C`; and `y` reaches `α ≠ d`, so `P₃` can run straight to `y`, with no `z`.
- **Theorem 3.** The first pass to one taxon per component isn't needed: removing a taxon or applying Lemma 3 covers every case. Checked in a scratch Lean file, outside the library.
