# Audit of TOB-QMC and its formalization

Paper: J. Dai, Y. Han, E. K. Molloy, *Quartet-based species tree methods enable fast and consistent tree of blobs
reconstruction under the network multispecies coalescent*,
[bioRxiv 10.1101/2025.11.05.686850 v5](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.05.686850v5), posted 2026-05-15, with its supplement
(internally dated February 26, 2026; its link to v5 is unconfirmed, and its numbering differs from v5's in places). A later journal version, [Genome Research, doi 10.1101/gr.282203.126](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.282203.126), could not be read in full (Section 10). No author TeX source exists, so the audit was made against the PDF of v5 and of the supplement. A line number is a locator, as [`docs/CONFIGURATION.md`](../../docs/CONFIGURATION.md) defines it: in the main text, a line number printed in the margin of the v5 PDF ("embedded lines 384-385", "v5, line 421"); in the supplement, a line of `pdftotext -layout` ("the supplement's text, lines 560-575"). The labels `tob2025:…` of the route check are this project's. Section, result and equation numbers are the paper's, and those of the supplement say so ("supplement Lemma 4").

The paper is one of four on the tree of blobs that one repository formalizes ([`../../README.md`](../../README.md)). Its findings keep the numbers they have across the collection: E18–E28, E39, E69–E74 and E83 (Section 3).

## 1. Summary

- **Errors.** The claims of the supplement that its Lemma 7 and Theorem 1 hold at arbitrary level are false at level 2 (E25), its Lemma 2's converse is false with `N|Y` the induced subnetwork, as it defines it (E23), and so is its claim that positive edge lengths exclude the centroid (E28). The margin of its Lemma 4 is vacuous as printed (E22). None of these affects a result of main v5: it states Theorem 2 at level 1, keeps only the forward direction of Lemma 2, and its Definition 1 excludes the centroid on T-quartets; supplement Lemma 4 holds with a repaired margin.
- **Gaps.** E18–E22, E24, E39. All are repaired. Only E22 changes a statement among these, the margin of supplement Lemma 4. In E21, the consistency claim needs the tests to be correct on treelike subsets that the tree of blobs does not resolve. The paper does not treat these, and they are proved here ([`TOBQMC.Level1.nmscCF_lt_of_not_displays`](../../src/Basic/Level1CF.lean#L172)).
- **Missing hypotheses.** Supplement Lemma 8 needs a nontrivial blob (E26, agreed with the user). Section 4.
- **Loose sentences and misprints.** E69–E74, loose sentences, most on running times, none affecting a result; among them, binary lifting isn't `O(1)` per query, though the total stands (E69). E27, a formula for `α` in §1.2 that conflicts with the later one. E34, the typos.
- **Redundant hypotheses.** Section 5. The most notable: all the refinement hypotheses in Lemma 4 except [`TOBQMC.RefinesBlob`](../../src/TOBQMC/Level1.lean#L40). Also `B` binary in Corollary 1, and, for the T3 option, 6a in full and 6b given condition 4.
- **Use of cited results.** TOB-QMC cites the statement of TINNiK's Theorem 4 for what is a step of its proof (E21). Its description of ASTRAL's search space is exact for ASTRAL-I's default only (Section 2). Huber et al.'s Theorem 5.1 and Ané et al.'s exchangeability are used correctly. Section 2 lists gaps in the proofs of cited results, and E82 and E83 are smaller points on cited works.

## 3. Errors and gaps in the paper

The findings are numbered across the papers that the repository formalizes. This paper's are E18–E28, E39, E69–E74 and E83. E82 and the last item of E34 are on Mitchell et al. 2019, whose T3 test the paper uses through TINNiK.

### Main text (v5)

**E18. Lemma 3, proof.** The result holds; the steps below are repaired in [`TOBQMC.lemma3_of_properties`](../../src/TOBQMC/WQC.lean#L619), [`TOBQMC.lemma3`](../../src/TOBQMC/WQC.lean#L678), and in the proof of exchangeability.

- Lines 449–451: "uniquely maximized by `T_N`" is wrong: every refinement of `T_N` ties ([`Phylogenetics.quartetPart_of_displays`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Detach.lean#L587)); (2) only compares with non-refinements. The strictness of (2) needs that a non-refinement differs from `T_N` on some T-quartet, so that displayed quartets determine splits ([`Phylogenetics.subset_of_forall_displays`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Splits.lean#L748)).
- Lines 467–471: "only after the four sampled lineages enter this blob can they coalesce" is false for the block containing the root: the three other lineages can coalesce inside the blob and then leave upward. The conclusion holds for a different reason: a pair that coalesces first decides the quartet whichever taxon is above, and if all three leave uncoalesced the three quartet trees are equally likely. [`Phylogenetics.NMSC.exchangeability`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Exchangeability.lean#L412) treats both cases.
- Lines 472–486: the reasons (i), (ii) justify `QS^v(new) = QS^v - QS^{v(b)} + QS^{v(a)}`, not the printed equality with `QS^v(T*)`; quartets without `b` are unaffected, and those with `b` read as the same with `a`, because `b` is now sister to `a`. These last facts are unstated, and the case `QS^{v(a)} > QS^{v(b)}` cannot occur at a maximizer.
- Line 485, "Repeating": needs three unstated facts: detaching `b` leaves the partial scores of the block's other taxa unchanged, so `a` stays best; the block side grows step by step; and earlier block sides
  survive later operations ([`TOBQMC.vertexTaxonPart_detachReattach_of_ne`](../../src/TOBQMC/WQC.lean#L331), [`Phylogenetics.insert_mem_detachReattach`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Detach.lean#L536), [`Phylogenetics.mem_detachReattach_of_not_mem`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Detach.lean#L549)). Also, `a` must be chosen afresh for each block, on the current tree.
- Lines 489–492: the reattached tree is a tree only because of property (2), since a side of the refined tree is either a lift or lies inside a block, which is unstated ([`Phylogenetics.reattach_isUnrootedTree`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Splice.lean#L723)). That a refinement gains needs that displayed quartets determine splits. "Amplified" has no single factor: the gain on `X` is the sum over representative tuples of the gains, divided by `∏|B|` ([`TOBQMC.prod_card_mul_vertexPart_sub`](../../src/TOBQMC/WQC.lean#L493)).
- Lines 492–495: property (2) is what forces every remaining side to be a union of blocks ([`TOBQMC.displays_filter_isBlockUnion`](../../src/TOBQMC/WQC.lean#L578)), which is not noted.
- Lines 497–498: compatibility of the local trees at different vertices is asserted ([`Phylogenetics.IsVertexPartition.compatible_of_ne`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Splice.lean#L78)), and that the result is binary needs a proof ([`Phylogenetics.isBinaryTree_of_forall_displays`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Detach.lean#L177)).

**E19. Lemma 4, proof.** Of ⇒ (lines 551–556): It does not treat two or more taxa below `v_h`. They lie in one block, a fact stated only in the proof of Corollary 1, so distinct blocks exclude this case. Of ⇐ (line 546): it stops at "these conditions are sufficient" without saying why the cycle with its attachments has no 2|2 cut edge. A cut edge isn't on the cycle, so two taxa on its far side would attach through it, contradicting distinct blocks ([`TOBQMC.Level1.not_hasWedgeCutSplit`](../../src/Basic/Wedge.lean#L1845)).
Lean: [`TOBQMC.Level1.blockWalk_of_below_reticulation`](../../src/Basic/Level1Blobs.lean#L903). The result holds.

**E20. Corollary 1, proof (lines 567–568), and Theorem 2, proof (lines 583–584).** "(otherwise `B_N` is not binary)" gives the wrong reason for each side having at least two blocks: a side with one block is a cut side, so the edge would be in the tree of blobs. Binary `B` is not needed. In Theorem 2, "By the setting" each block lies in a single block of the quadripartition also needs `e ∉ T_N`, for the same reason ([`TOBQMC.Level1.exists_not_isTreelike`](../../src/TOBQMC/Level1.lean#L457)). That an edge of `B_N` not in `T_N` refines one vertex of `T_N`, from a non-trivial blob (lines 530–531, 563–564), is also taken "by the setting": a lowest bridge with taxa of both sides below it gives the vertex, and a trivial blob has too few children for four blocks ([`TOBQMC.exists_blob_of_false_positive`](../../src/TOBQMC/Level1.lean#L329)). The result holds.

**E21. Consistency (lines 597–600).** The paper cites TINNiK's Theorem 4 as stating that the tests are correct on all four-taxon subsets. Its statement is about the algorithm's output, and this fact is a step of its proof, for generic parameters at `ρ = 0`, so it doesn't apply as cited under v5's assumptions. The fact does hold under them, for reasons the paper does not give: non-treelike subsets by 6a; subsets the tree of blobs resolves by nonanomaly and exchangeability; and for a treelike subset the tree of blobs does not resolve, a highest separating arc is not a bridge, and its source is a fork where the rest of the process is symmetric, so the split's CF is strictly largest ([`TOBQMC.Level1.nmscCF_lt_of_not_displays`](../../src/Basic/Level1CF.lean#L172), from [`Phylogenetics.NMSC.nmscCF_lt_of_fork`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/TreeCF.lean#L451)). Such a subset need not induce a tree: a reticulation above the fork can stay in its induced subgraph, with three lineages through it. So at level 1, T-quartet nonanomaly implies condition 6b, and [`TOBQMC.Level1.cfDetectable`](../../src/TOBQMC/Level1CF.lean#L54) gives CF-detectability under v5's assumptions. The step from Theorem 1's almost-sure "for all large `k`" to probability tending to one is unstated. Lean: [`TOBQMC.consistency`](../../src/TOBQMC/Consistency.lean#L131), [`TINNiK.tests_correct`](../../src/TINNiK/Basic.lean#L506), and the private [`tendsto_optimaRefine`](../../src/TOBQMC/Consistency.lean#L78) of [`src/TOBQMC/Consistency.lean`](../../src/TOBQMC/Consistency.lean).

**E22. Margin (lines 503–505, and supplement Lemma 4, Eq. (1)).** "`π` is the minimum absolute difference between the qCFs of T-quartets" is always zero: by exchangeability every T-quartet has equal alternatives, so `π(Y) = 0`, and the supplement's "`π_min > 0`" is false for every network whose tree of blobs is not a star. The condition "absolute error `< 0`" is impossible, so Lemma 4 as printed is vacuous. Lean: [`TOBQMC.not_printedPiMin_pos`](../../src/TOBQMC/SupplementLemma4Margin.lean#L65), [`TOBQMC.not_exists_printedError_lt`](../../src/TOBQMC/SupplementLemma4Margin.lean#L73). Repair: use the positive differences between the displayed quartet and each alternative, minimized over T-quartets, with the same radius. Lean: [`TOBQMC.Margin`](../../src/TOBQMC/WQC.lean#L691), [`TOBQMC.exists_margin`](../../src/TOBQMC/WQC.lean#L857).

## 6. How the formalization reads the paper

### Networks, trees, the coalescent and the tests

These readings are shared with ECToBlob and TINNiK, which the repository formalizes on the same definitions ([ECToBlob](../ECToBlob/README.md), [TINNiK](../TINNiK/README.md)).

- **Networks** are directed multigraphs with named arcs ([`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Network/Defs.lean#L30)), rooted, with parallel arcs allowed. The papers' semi-directed networks are handled through the rooted networks they come from; TOB-QMC's `N⁻` is `N` with a root.
- **Unrooted trees** are families of sides of splits ([`Phylogenetics.IsUnrootedTree`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Defs.lean#L49): [`unroot`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Hierarchy/Defs.lean#L43) of a hierarchy). The tree of blobs `T_B(N)` is the family of nonempty proper sides of cut edges ([`Phylogenetics.treeOfBlobs`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Network/Quartets.lean#L40)).
  Bridges to the graphs: [`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork.blobTree`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Network/TreeOfBlobs.lean#L127) is the graph with blobs contracted; [`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork.mem_treeOfBlobs_iff_blobTree`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Network/TreeOfBlobs.lean#L213) says the two agree on splits, and [`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork.blobTree_isTree`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Network/TreeOfBlobs.lean#L134) that it is a tree. The root of `blobTree` is not deleted. Allman et al.'s [`AllmanEtAl2023.Trd`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobDefinitions.lean#L422) is the reduced graph, and [`AllmanEtAl2023.Bridges.splits_Trd`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Bridges.lean#L353) says its splits are this family.
- **Induced networks**: `HasCutSplitOn Y A` says that one arc of the subgraph of ancestors of `Y` is the only arc of it crossing a vertex set that meets `Y` in `A`; [`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork.hasCutSplitOn_iff_separates`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Network/Cuts.lean#L253) gives the domination form, and [`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork.hasCutSplitOn_iff_inducedNetwork_hasCutSplit`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Network/Induced.lean#L857) proves it equal to the cut splits of the graph `inducedNetwork` (LSA restriction, then suppression). TOB-QMC uses this network, whose CFs are those of `N` on four taxa ([`Phylogenetics.NMSC.nmscCF_inducedNetwork`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Induced.lean#L76)). Allman et al.'s own results use their Definition 6, [`AllmanEtAl2023.induced`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/InducedNetwork.lean#L296), which keeps what lies above the LSA; the two have the same cut-edge splits ([`AllmanEtAl2023.Bridges.induced_hasCutSplit_iff`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Bridges.lean#L210)).
- **Quartet trees** are unordered pairs of cherries; [`Phylogenetics.Displays`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Defs.lean#L84).
- **The NMSC** on arbitrary networks is an order-free sum over histories ([`Phylogenetics.geneTreeDist`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Defs.lean#L94)), the product over vertices and arcs of the joint law of the forests at the bottom and top of each arc. One lineage per taxon. `ρ` is the inheritance correlation of TOB-QMC's supplement, in closed form as an exchangeable Pólya law ([`Phylogenetics.allocationWeight`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Defs.lean#L54), proved equal to the urn in [`Phylogenetics.allocationWeight_eq_urn`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Inheritance.lean#L232)). [`Phylogenetics.sum_geneTreeDist`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Basic.lean#L51) and [`Phylogenetics.geneTreeDist_support`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Basic.lean#L64) validate it: a law on binary rooted trees.
- **Sampling**: `Phylogenetics.iidProb dist n Ev` is the finite sum over samples, proved equal to the product measure ([`Phylogenetics.iidProb_eq_pi`](../../src/Basic/Testing/IID.lean#L264)). TOB-QMC's almost-sure statement uses any probability space (`TOBQMC.IsGeneTreeSample`).
- **Test statistics** follow the implementation (MSCquartets, Mitchell et al.). The T1 p-value uses the plug-in `min(1, φ̂)` at all counts; small-count procedures of software are not modelled. Star: likelihood ratio by default, Pearson as `StarTest.pearson`.
- **Parameters**: `MetricData` has lengths on all arcs and hybridization parameters; `IsMetric` requires positivity on tree edges, and `γ` strictly between 0 and 1 where a reticulation has two parents.
- **The T3 test**: [`Phylogenetics.t3Stat`](../../src/External/MitchellAllmanRhodes2019/T3Test.lean#L56) is the minimum over topologies of [`t1Stat`](../../src/Basic/Testing/Defs.lean#L63), and [`Phylogenetics.t3Var`](../../src/External/MitchellAllmanRhodes2019/T3Test.lean#L63) the random variable of Mitchell et al.'s Proposition T3approx, whose squared distance to the three half-lines is [`Phylogenetics.QuartetTests.t3Var_eq_min_sqDistHalfLine`](../../src/External/MitchellAllmanRhodes2019/T3Test.lean#L172) (a step they leave as "it can be shown"); at `Z = 0` their formula gives 0, harmless since `Z = 0` has probability zero. The sources differ at the centroid: Mitchell et al.'s null includes it, TINNiK's Fig. 2 caption excludes it, and its anomalous condition `q ≥ 1/3` counts it as anomalous. Lean follows each: [`Phylogenetics.IsT3Null`](../../src/External/MitchellAllmanRhodes2019/T3Test.lean#L51) follows Mitchell et al., `TINNiK.NoAnomalousQuartets` TINNiK. TINNiK's Theorem 4 says "using the cut test", so [`TINNiK.theorem4T3`](../../src/TINNiK/T3.lean#L469) has no printed proof to follow, and takes the structure of the proof for the cut test.
- **Counts of operations** are counts of what the paper's claims are about, tied to the definitions by `Finset.card` of what a step enumerates. There is no machine model, so these aren't running times. [`TOBQMC.threeFixOneAlterOperations_le`](../../src/TOBQMC/Counts.lean#L85) takes the LCA cost and the precomputation as parameters, and [`TOBQMC.threeFixOneAlterReads_le`](../../src/TOBQMC/RunningTime.lean#L57) fills them in with a sparse table (E69). Some costs are charged and not counted: TREE-QMC's cost per subproblem comes from its Theorems 1–2 ([`TOBQMC.treeQMCWork_le`](../../src/TOBQMC/RunningTime.lean#L129)); and ASTRAL-III's heuristics are parameters with the bounds it states ([`TOBQMC.card_astral3Space_le`](../../src/TOBQMC/RunningTime.lean#L512)). Linear-time blobs and bridges are [`Phylogenetics.BridgeDFS.isBridge_iff`](../../src/Basic/BridgeDFS.lean#L854) and [`Phylogenetics.BridgeDFS.count_eq`](../../src/Basic/BridgeDFS.lean#L906), a search by low-points in `|V| + 2 |E|` steps, and [`Phylogenetics.BridgeDFS.mem_treeOfBlobs_iff`](../../src/Basic/BridgeDFS.lean#L1222) reads the tree of blobs from it as at most `|V|` intervals of discovery times.

### Before the paper

- Gusfield, Bansal, Bafna, Song, [J. Comput. Biol. 14 (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1089/cmb.2006.0137): a decomposition theorem for phylogenetic networks that exposes "a maximal embedded tree structure". TOB-QMC cites it for the tree of blobs. It is formalized separately, in [lean4-gusfield2007](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-gusfield2007).
- Solís-Lemus, Ané, [PLoS Genet. 12 (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1005896) (SNaQ, pseudolikelihood on quartets); Baños, [Bull. Math. Biol. 81 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-018-0485-4) ([arXiv 2017-11-28](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10545); read for its Proposition 7): "every cycle of size at least 4 and every hybrid node in a cycle of size at least 5" of a level-1 network are identifiable from gene tree quartets; Allman, Baños, Rhodes, [NANUQ, Algorithms Mol. Biol. 14 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13015-019-0159-2) ([arXiv 2019-05-16](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07050)): level-1 inference from quartet hypothesis tests and a quartet intertaxon distance. TINNiK's introduction sets its algorithm against these level-1 methods.
- Allman, Baños, Mitchell, Rhodes, [J. Math. Biol. 86 (2023), 10](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-022-01838-9) ([arXiv 2022-05-06](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03466)): "most features of the unrooted tree of blobs" are determined by the distribution of gene quartet topologies, with no assumption on the level, through a B-quartet inference rule. It names the difficulty that TOB-QMC, TINNiK and ECToBlob meet in different ways: a B-quartet of the network can have a cut-edge split on its induced 4-taxon network, "the main obstacle". It is a primary paper of the repository: see [its folder](../AllmanEtAl2023/README.md).
- Allman, Baños, Mitchell, Rhodes, TINNiK, [Algorithms Mol. Biol. 19 (2024), 23](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13015-024-00266-2) ([bioRxiv 2024-04-24](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.04.20.590418)): turns the sketch of Allman et al. 2023 into an algorithm with a consistency theorem (Theorem 4), and writes "We know of no other proposed algorithm for tree of blobs inference from biological data"; TOB-QMC describes it as the first. TOB-QMC uses its hypothesis tests (Section 2). It is a primary paper of the repository: see [its folder](../TINNiK/README.md).
- Ané, Fogg, Allman, Baños, Rhodes, [J. Math. Biol. 88 (2024), 29](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-024-02050-7) ([bioRxiv 2023-08-21](https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.08.18.553582)): under common inheritance "4-taxon networks are never anomalous"; under independent and correlated inheritance simulations find "truly anomalous networks are rare" and many networks close to the anomaly zone. Source of TOB-QMC's no-anomaly hypotheses, of its Lemma 1, and of the exchangeability of Section 2.
- Dinh, Baños, [Mol. Biol. Evol. 42 (2025), msaf049](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msaf049) (published 2025-03-07): under the NMSC, "including nonanomalous scenarios", ASTRAL can produce a tree "that does not correspond to any topology displayed by the true underlying network". TOB-QMC cites it, with Molloy, Durvasula, Sankararaman 2021 (its [12], [27]).
- Huber, van Iersel, Jones, Moulton, Veenema-Nipius, [Bull. Math. Biol. 87 (2025), 136](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-025-01510-5) ([arXiv 2024-08-23](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12997)). Theorem 5.1, for binary semi-directed networks, which "does not put any restriction on the level": a nontrivial `A|B` is a cut-edge split exactly when `a₁a₂|b₁b₂` is a cut-edge split of the quarnet for all `a₁, a₂ ∈ A`, `b₁, b₂ ∈ B`. Corollary 5.6: the quarnets determine the blob tree. TOB-QMC's Lemma 2 is the forward direction, as TOB-QMC says.
- Frohn, Holtgrefe, van Iersel, Jones, Kelk, [J. Comput. Syst. Sci. 152 (2025), 103655](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcss.2025.103655) ([arXiv 2024-09-09](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06034)). Lemma 1 sharpens Huber et al.'s theorem by fixing `a₁` and `b₁`. Theorem 5 reconstructs the tree of blobs of a binary network of any level in `O(n³)` time from `O(n³)` quarnet-splits, and Proposition 6 gives a lower bound of `Ω(n log n + ℓn)` quarnet-splits at level `ℓ`.

Huber et al. and Frohn et al. work from exact quarnet information, with no statistical model. Both appeared after the TINNiK preprint and do not build on its algorithm.

What the paper adds. TOB-QMC and ECToBlob add a different outline: an optimum of the quartet criterion refines the tree of blobs, and edges are then contracted by quartet tests. We found no earlier statement of that refinement result. Section 2 lists the cited results the proofs use.

## 11. What's next

### Open directions

- **Treelikeness beyond level 1.** ECToBlob's §10 says of the T3 test: "we know of no proof of a stronger result that would justify using the T3 test", and that the formal justification for TOB-QMC stops at level 1; Section 10 finds no published work that settles this. The question: for a network of any level and a refinement `B` of its tree of blobs, does every edge of `B` not in the tree of blobs have a non-treelike 4-taxon set among those around it?

### Simpler proofs

Each of these is, or was in an earlier version, the argument of the formal proof, so it was checked in Lean. We found none of them published, in the search of Section 10.

- **TOB-QMC's ASTRAL corollary.** Every binary gene tree occurs almost surely, so all binary trees are in the search space; there's no need for the probability of a bipartition.
- **TOB-QMC's supplement Lemma 8.** A lowest reticulation of the blob has only bridges below it, in any phylogenetic network.
- **The T3 test at the singularity.** Non-collapse needs no limit law: the T3 statistic is at most the T1 statistic, which is tight on the null, and the p-value is at least `½ χ²₁(10 t)`, since `sin² α₀ ≥ 1/10` on `[0,1]`.
- **TOB-QMC Lemma 3, the second observation.** Lines 489–492 restrict to one taxon per block and say a refinement's gain "would be amplified after reattaching". No restriction is needed: a maximizer resolves every 4-set in distinct blocks, since adding one split that resolves one more gains a positive CF on `X` directly, and that the result is binary then follows from the standard fact that a tree is binary exactly when it resolves every 4-set ([`Phylogenetics.isBinaryTree_of_forall_displays`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Detach.lean#L177), [`Phylogenetics.IsBinaryTree.exists_displays`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/f1a3592dee6e3f7d5e7a68a0695ac457de23d2de/Phylogenetics/Tree/Splits.lean#L589)). An earlier version of the formal proof did this (in the repository's history); the formal proof now follows the paper. Similarly, the contradiction at lines 472–486 adds nothing to the direct transformation it contains.
- **TOB-QMC Lemma 4.** Read on the rooted induced subgraph, with no wedge paths: for ⇐, a blob arc does not dominate the taxon below the reticulation, and an arc away from the blob has the whole blob below it or two taxa in distinct blocks joined outside it; for ⇒, descend from the top of the blob to an arc with exactly two taxa below it. An earlier version of the formal proof did this (in the repository's history); the formal proof now follows the paper, through wedge paths and the bridge [`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork.hasWedgeCutSplit_iff_hasCutSplitOn`](../../src/Basic/Wedge.lean#L901).

To the library `Phylogenetics`: `RhoOne`, the strict form of Ané et al.'s Proposition 12; `LCA`, the sparse table and binary lifting of E69; `BridgeDFS`, bridges and blobs in linear time; `Level1Blobs` and `Wedge`, blobs of level-1 networks and the wedge paths of Lemma 4; `InducedSplits`, `TreeCounts` and `UnitMetric`. To Mathlib: `ForMathlib/*`, the analysis and probability behind the tests. Staying in this project: `Defs`, with TOB-QMC's algorithm and standing assumptions; `Testing/*`; `LocalObjective`; `Level1Blocks`, with two of the three cases of Lemma 4; `Level1CF`, with the strictly largest concordance factor of E21; and `FinNetwork`. What is general to phylogenetics is already in the library [`lean4-phylogenetics`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics), which keeps what is standard to the field.

[`src/External/MitchellAllmanRhodes2019/`](../../src/External/MitchellAllmanRhodes2019) has the limit laws and densities of the T3 and T1 tests. These are for other formalizations citing the same sources.
