# Audit of Allman et al. 2023

- **Allman et al. 2023**: E. S. Allman, H. Baños, J. D. Mitchell, J. A. Rhodes, *The tree of blobs of a species network:
  identifiability under the coalescent*, [J. Math. Biol. 86 (2023), 10](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-022-01838-9), read in the
  PMC author manuscript NIHMS1884071 ([PMC10062380](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10062380/)) and the
  [arXiv TeX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03466). It is a primary paper here, and the three above also cite it.

This is the part of [the audit](../REPORT.md) about Allman et al. 2023. [The audit](../REPORT.md) has what the five papers have in common: the summary and the status of the formalization, the results from prior work that they cite (Section 2), the literature (Section 10) and what's next (Section 11). The sections here keep its numbers, and the findings are numbered across the five papers: here E55–E66.

## 3. Errors and gaps in the paper

"Line N" here is a line of the plain text of the PMC page of the author manuscript NIHMS1884071, as in [`docs/CONFIGURATION.md`](../CONFIGURATION.md) ("the PMC text, line N"). This manuscript and the arXiv TeX were both read, and the journal's text wasn't reachable, so nothing here was checked against it. The items in the manuscript only are marked; the rest are in the TeX as well. The later papers cite this one, and none of their uses is affected (Section 2). No numbered result of the paper fails, reading a subnetwork as connected; read literally, Lemma 2 does (E55). E57 and E64 record sentences that fail as read literally, and the rest are gaps, loose sentences and misprints.

**E55. Lemma 2 (a missing word; the statement needs it).** Line 184: "Consider a network N with degree-1 nodes bijectively labelled by X, and a subnetwork M of N with the restricted labelling of some degree-1 nodes by Y ⊆ X. If a set S ⊆ Y determines a blob on M, then S determines a blob on N." Lemma 2 needs `M` connected, or at least each `s` joined to `ℬ₀` in `M`. Its proof assumes this and all its uses satisfy it, but the word is missing. For disconnected `M`, Definition 8 can hold vacuously and the conclusion fails. No result of the paper is affected. The paper writes "connected subnetwork" where it means that (line 207), so the literal reading is available. The counterexample is the caterpillar `(((a, b), c), d)` with `M` the pendant edges: [`AllmanEtAl2023.lemma2_needs_connected`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Lemma2Counterexample.lean#L163). [`AllmanEtAl2023.lemma2`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Lemma2.lean#L146) takes `M` connected, which is what its uses have ([`AllmanEtAl2023.isConnectedSubnetwork_ancestralArcs`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Corollary2.lean#L43)).

**E56. Lemma 2: the degree-one hypothesis, and blobs of three taxa (gaps).** "With degree-1 nodes bijectively labelled by X" is more than the uses satisfy: at line 215 Lemma 3 applies Lemma 2 with `M` in the role of `N`, and the root of `M` can have degree 1 there, with no label. The proof doesn't use it: [`AllmanEtAl2023.IsLabelledNetwork`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Lemma2.lean#L30) asks for distinct labels on nodes of degree 1. In the proof, "it has a subpath from ℬ to s entirely outside of ℬ" (line 185) needs `s ∉ ℬ`, which holds since a degree-one node is alone in its blob, and such a blob isn't determined by three or more labels. And line 73, "on a network every subset of 3 taxa determines a blob", has no proof: [`AllmanEtAl2023.BlobStructure.existsUnique_isDeterminedBy_three`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobStructure.lean#L305), through [`AllmanEtAl2023.exists_median`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/SubgraphBlobs.lean#L588).

**E57. Proposition 1 (four small points; the proposition holds as printed).**

- **Line 165: the lengths (a misprint, manuscript only).** "the pendant edges to a, b, c, d to be of lengths 1, 1, 1 + (k + 1)ϵ + (2^k − 1)M, respectively" gives three lengths for four edges. The TeX has `1, 1, 1 + (k+1)ε, 2 + (k+1)ε + (2^k − 1)M`, and Figure 6 agrees. Lean uses those ([`AllmanEtAl2023.NetworkNk.metricNk`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/NetworkNk.lean#L135)), which make the network ultrametric as the sentence says ([`AllmanEtAl2023.NetworkNk.heightNk_source`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/NetworkNkGraph.lean#L791)).
- **Line 166: "Note that by Theorem 1, CF_abcd is strictly (ab|cd)-cut for ϵ > 0" (false as a claim on all `ε > 0`; unused).** Theorem 1 is generic, and the proposition's own intermediate-value argument reaches `(1/3, 1/3, 1/3)`: [`AllmanEtAl2023.NetworkNk.exists_not_isStrictlyCutAtFirst`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Proposition1.lean#L229). No step uses the sentence.
- **Lines 163, 167, 173: `p` means two things (notation).** It is an entry of the triple in the statement, and `exp(-ϵ)` in the proof; the last paragraph writes `(q, p, p)`, `q + 2p = 1`, for the statement's `(p, q, q)`.
- **Line 164: "By statement 1 of Theorem 1" (a loose citation that holds).** That statement is for generic parameters, and the proposition is for all valid ones. Generic parameters are dense and CFs are continuous, so the citation is good: [`AllmanEtAl2023.ForGenericParams.eq_zero_of_continuous`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/GenericDense.lean#L24), [`AllmanEtAl2023.proposition1`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Proposition1.lean#L62).

**E58. Theorem 1, statement (b) (gaps; the result holds).**

- **Lines 147–148: "suppressing any degree-2 nodes" isn't enough.** A root can lose a child, and then it has out-degree 1, which Definition 1 excludes. On the 4-cycle with arcs `r→v, r→x, x→b, x→y, y→c, y→z, z→d, z→v, v→a`, deleting `r→v` does this. Step 3 of Definition 6 is what's needed, and the CFs are unaffected ([`AllmanEtAl2023.nmscCF_removeStem`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/InducedCF.lean#L470)).
- **Line 149: "Now e must be a cut edge in both N1 and N2" has no reason.** It's true: a cut edge of `Nᵢ` with split `ab|cd` lies in `N₃` between `ℬ` and `b`, so the attachments of `a` lie beyond it, and hence beyond `e` ([`AllmanEtAl2023.IsLowPair.ends_mem_far`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Reduction.lean#L277), [`AllmanEtAl2023.IsLowPair.onlyCrosses_far_insert`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Reduction.lean#L337)).
- **Line 146: "Doing so, we have effectively removed these blobs"** has no reason ([`AllmanEtAl2023.nmscCF_contracted`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ContractBlob.lean#L724), [`AllmanEtAl2023.exists_isContractible_of_cutDegree_two`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ContractBlob.lean#L1080)). **Line 148: "since 𝒩− has no 3-blobs"** leaves unproved the 3-blob at the parent of a cherry ([`AllmanEtAl2023.exists_cutDegree_three_of_cherry`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem1b.lean#L289)).
- **Implicit:** that a lowest hybrid node exists, and how generic parameters on `𝒩⁺` reach the `𝒩ᵢ⁺` at line 152, which states the choice with no reason ([`AllmanEtAl2023.measure_deleted_preimage`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem1b.lean#L82)).

**E59. Corollary 1 (a gap; the result holds).** Line 157, "they give rise to a generic condition on numerical parameter values on the n-taxon network", needs that the map of parameters to an induced network sends valid parameters to valid ones, and that preimages of null sets are null: [`AllmanEtAl2023.isMetric_inducedParams`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ClaimsTheorem1.lean#L52), [`AllmanEtAl2023.isGenericNull_inducedParams`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ClaimsTheorem1.lean#L73). It also needs the identity of the CFs, which line 181 states "by the structure of the NMSC model": [`AllmanEtAl2023.nmscCF_induced`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/ClaimsTheorem1.lean#L36).

**E60. Corollary 2 and Lemma 3: the stem (a gap, manuscript only).** Line 186 says that induced networks "are obtained from subnetworks by suppressing degree-2 nodes", and line 207 likewise. The manuscript's Definition 6 has a third step that removes a stem from the root, and neither passage mentions it. (The TeX's Definition 6 has only the first two steps.) Blobs and cut edges pass through it: [`AllmanEtAl2023.Bridges.isBlobQuartetOnInduced_iff`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Bridges.lean#L636), [`AllmanEtAl2023.Bridges.induced_hasCutSplit_iff`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Bridges.lean#L210).

**E61. Lemma 3 (omitted steps; the result holds).**

- Line 208: "Thus e0 also separates a, b, from c, d" has no reason: the side containing `c, d` avoids `ℬ′` and is connected.
- Line 213: "Thus there is a path P3 from e0 to z in K_cd": the reason gives connectivity in `N′` only, and the path has to be kept in `K_cd`.
- Line 214: "This cycle also lies in M, as none of its edges are ancestral only to the taxon d. Since ℬ′ also lies in M" has no argument for `K_ab`, and doesn't follow from `ℬ′` being a blob of `N′` alone. It holds because `ℬ′ ⊆ K_ab` and `d ∈ K_cd`: a directed path from `K_ab` to `d` must use `e₀`, whose head is above all of `K_cd`, so it can also lead to `c` ([`AllmanEtAl2023.CutEdgeData.near_reaches`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/CycleLemma.lean#L422)).
- Line 214: that `C` "passes through A, Kab, e0, and Kcd" after removing self-intersections, and that the path from `z` to `y` avoids `e₀`, so that `e₀` is used once, are unstated.
- Line 215: "and hence in M ∩ N′" goes from a larger network to a smaller one, the direction Lemma 2 doesn't give. It holds because the edges above `d` alone lie in `K_cd`, so `ℬ′` is untouched ([`AllmanEtAl2023.CutEdgeData.determines_off`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/CycleLemma.lean#L870)).
- Line 215: "the initial segment of P1 gives a path from α to C" needs `P₁` to meet `C` after the removal. It does: take the last vertex of `P₁` on the directed path from `y` to `α` ([`AllmanEtAl2023.CutEdgeData.Paths.exists_meet`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/CycleLemma.lean#L692)).

**E62. Theorem 3 (omitted steps; the result holds).** Line 222, "Passing to the induced network on those taxa, all edges in ℬ are retained", has no proof ([`AllmanEtAl2023.exists_representatives_induced`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Claims.lean#L129)). The induction at lines 222 and 225 speaks of the induced 4-taxon networks of an induced network, and needs these to have the quartet topologies of the 4-taxon networks induced from `𝒩⁺`, which the paper doesn't say: [`AllmanEtAl2023.inducedHasQuartetTopology_induced`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Claims.lean#L106), [`AllmanEtAl2023.inducedHasStarTopology_induced`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Claims.lean#L117).

**E63. The caption of Figure 5 (a labelling slip, manuscript only).** The caption at line 293 has `𝒩₁⁺` "the result of further removing edge h₂", and `𝒩₂⁺` from removing `h₁`. The formula at line 151 is `CF(𝒩⁺) = γ₁ CF(𝒩₂⁺) + γ₂ CF(𝒩₁⁺)`, with `γ₁, γ₂` the parameters of `h₁, h₂`, which fits `𝒩ᵢ⁺` removing `hᵢ`, as line 148 has it. Only the labels are affected. The formula is in the TeX too, and the figure and its caption are not. In Lean the two networks are named by the arc deleted ([`AllmanEtAl2023.InPassing.cf_mixture`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/InPassing.lean#L550)).

**E64. Line 252: edges between trivial blobs (an implicit qualification).** "For edges between trivial blobs, it is straightforward to see that edge lengths are identifiable." Read literally, this fails for pendant edges: a taxon and its parent can both be trivial blobs, and a single lineage on the edge never coalesces, so nothing depends on its length ([`AllmanEtAl2023.FourCycle.pendant_not_identifiable`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/FourCycle.lean#L817), [`AllmanEtAl2023.FourCycle.isTrivialBlob_root_taxon`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/FourCycle.lean#L823)). At the root of `𝒩⊕`, only the sum of the two lengths counts. Probably a missing word. The proved form is for internal edges of `𝒩⁻`: [`AllmanEtAl2023.TrivialEdges.semidirected_length_eq`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/TrivialEdges.lean#L696), with [`AllmanEtAl2023.TrivialEdges.edge_length_eq`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/TrivialEdges.lean#L487) and [`AllmanEtAl2023.TrivialEdges.root_length_eq`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/TrivialEdges.lean#L549) on `𝒩⁺`.

**E65. Line 252: "k or M varied" (loose).** "If the length of the edge between the blobs is held fixed but k or M varied, the CF varies over a line segment." Since `k` is discrete, the sentence needs `M` varied at each `k` ([`AllmanEtAl2023.InPassing.isPreconnected_cf_first`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobDistance.lean#L64)), or `k` and `M` varied together ([`AllmanEtAl2023.InPassing.isPreconnected_iUnion_cf_first`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobDistance.lean#L197)), and both hold. The conclusion is proved on one `𝒩(k)⁺`, for every `k ≥ 1`, every `ε₀ > 0` and all `ε′` near it: two nearby values share a nondegenerate segment of CFs ([`AllmanEtAl2023.InPassing.blobDistance_not_identifiable`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobDistance.lean#L115)), from the exact first entry of the CF ([`NetworkNk.cf_first_eq`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/NetworkNkExact.lean#L1221)) and its limits at `M → 0⁺` and `M → ∞` ([`NetworkNk.cfZero`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/NetworkNkExact.lean#L1236), [`NetworkNk.cfInfty`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/NetworkNkExact.lean#L1239)).

**E66. Misprints in the proof of Lemma 3.** Line 208: "K_bc" for `K_cd`, in the TeX as well (its line 1087). In the manuscript only: line 208, "N \ {e0}" for `N′ \ {e₀}`, and line 207, "𝒩⁺, ℳ⁺" for `𝒩′, ℳ⁺`.

## 6. How the formalization reads the paper

- **The paper's objects.** The results as printed are on [`AllmanEtAl2023.IsBinaryNetwork`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Definitions.lean#L61), its Definition 1 verbatim, tied to the library's definitions by [`AllmanEtAl2023.isBinaryNetwork_iff`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Definitions.lean#L139). [`AllmanEtAl2023.Trd`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobDefinitions.lean#L422) is `T_rd(𝒩⁻)` as a labelled graph, and [`AllmanEtAl2023.Bridges.splits_Trd`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Bridges.lean#L353) says its splits are the library's [`Phylogenetics.treeOfBlobs`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/9948de9b3cba330596424efd2dca9617be2efb0c/Phylogenetics/Network/Quartets.lean#L40). The topologies of Figure 4 are listed literally by `HasQuartetTopology` and `HasStarTopology` on [`AllmanEtAl2023.LabelledGraph`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobDefinitions.lean#L385).
- **The manuscript and the TeX.** Definition 6 follows the manuscript, with its third step that removes a stem ([`AllmanEtAl2023.induced`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/InducedNetwork.lean#L296)). The lengths of `𝒩(k)⁺` follow the TeX and Figure 6, and the manuscript's lengths are a misprint (E57). The two versions also differ in Definition 8's wording, with the same meaning; the manuscript's fuller proof of Theorem 1 (b), with `w`, `k₁`, `k₂`, Figure 5 and "only a single edge below v"; its misprints in the proof of Lemma 3 (E66); and more detail on the algorithm in Section 5. A comparison word by word found nothing else mathematical.
- **"Identifiable from `CF_Q`" (Corollary 1).** [`AllmanEtAl2023.readTopology`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L293), a fixed function of the triple, returns the topology of `T_rd(𝒩⁻_Q)` for generic parameters, with one exceptional set per network for all `Q`.
- **"Can be identified" (Theorem 3).** [`AllmanEtAl2023.CFInferred`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L319) is a predicate on the CFs alone: what [`readTopology`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L293) reads as unresolved, closed under the printed rule, whose third hypothesis is read off the CF of the third set. [`AllmanEtAl2023.theorem3`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem3.lean#L510) says that it holds exactly on the B-quartets, for generic parameters; the direction from [`CFInferred`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L319) to B-quartet, which "identified" needs, is Theorem 2.
- **"Identifiable" (Theorem 4).** There is one function `F` of the CFs, chosen before the network, with `F(CF)` the splits of `T_rd(𝒩⁻)` off a null set of each network's parameters ([`AllmanEtAl2023.theorem4`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem4.lean#L103)). A function that may depend on the network would say nothing. [`AllmanEtAl2023.theorem4_treeFromCF`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem4.lean#L86) names the function, [`AllmanEtAl2023.theorem4_two_networks`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem4.lean#L111) is the pairwise form, where the null set may depend on the other network, and [`AllmanEtAl2023.theorem4_iso`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Theorem4.lean#L134) the labelled-graph form.
- **"Arises" (Proposition 1).** A network of Definition 1 with parameters of Definition 3. The proof gives more, `𝒩(k)⁺` for all large `k`: [`AllmanEtAl2023.NetworkNk.exists_params`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Proposition1.lean#L193).
- **Generic parameters.** [`AllmanEtAl2023.ForGenericParams`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L282) is the library's [`Phylogenetics.RootedNetwork.IsGenericNull`](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/9948de9b3cba330596424efd2dca9617be2efb0c/Phylogenetics/NMSC/Defs.lean#L122), Lebesgue-null in the paper's coordinates, and it isn't vacuous ([`AllmanEtAl2023.not_isGenericNull_isMetric`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/GenericParams.lean#L70)). One exceptional set per network serves all namings of the taxa and all 4-sets, which is stronger than Theorem 1's statement and equivalent by a finite union.
- **Lemma 2** names the blob on `N` as the one containing the blob on `M`. **Corollary 2** has `Q ⊆ Y` explicit. **Theorem 2** has `n ≥ 5` as five distinct taxa. **Lemma 3** writes out that `α` is not one of `a, b, c, d`.
- **In passing.** `IsAboveLSA` and `IsBelowLSA` (in [`AllmanEtAl2023.BlobStructure`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/BlobStructure.lean)) and `LiesIn` for "the blob gives a node" are ours. "6-blob when unrooted" in Figure 9R is read as six taxa determining a blob of the edges above them.
- **The outline in Section 5.** [`Algorithm.outline`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Algorithm.lean#L59) is its steps for any test, and [`Algorithm.outline_consistent`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Algorithm.lean#L232) says that the output is consistent when the test is right on each set of four taxa. With the cut test it is TINNiK's algorithm ([`TINNiK.outline_cut_eq_tinnik`](../../src/TINNiK/AllmanOutline.lean#L45)). The sentence "Like NANUQ, it can be shown" is in the manuscript only, with no proof.
- **Baños's range (line 158).** It is read as the values that arise, on one level-1 network: [`NetworkNk.banos_range`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/NetworkOne.lean#L795), from the exact first entry on `𝒩(1)⁺` ([`NetworkNk.nmscCF_one`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/NetworkOne.lean#L410)). Baños's bound on all level-1 networks with a `3₂`-cycle isn't proved, and nothing uses it.
- **The forms for the later papers** are stated in another language, on subgraphs of a fixed network and by domination: [`AllmanEtAl2023.determines_of_subnetwork`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/SubgraphBlobs.lean#L479), [`AllmanEtAl2023.rule_core`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Basic.lean#L614), [`AllmanEtAl2023.lemma3_resolves`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Basic.lean#L240), [`AllmanEtAl2023.closure_complete`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Basic.lean#L724), [`AllmanEtAl2023.cfDetectable_generic`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/CFDetectable.lean#L24). [`AllmanEtAl2023.corollary1`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Corollary1.lean#L67) doesn't imply [`AllmanEtAl2023.cfDetectable_generic`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/CFDetectable.lean#L24) by its statement alone: [`readTopology`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Concordance.lean#L293) reads an all-equal CF as unresolved, while [`AllmanEtAl2023.CFDetectable`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/CFDetectable.lean) asks for a non-cut CF there. So that one comes from the step of the proof on one 4-taxon network, [`AllmanEtAl2023.forGenericParams_inducedDetectable`](../../src/AllmanEtAl2023/Corollary1.lean#L39).
