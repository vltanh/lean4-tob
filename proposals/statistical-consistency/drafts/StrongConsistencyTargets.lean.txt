SCALAR ANALYTIC TARGETS

   deterministic stability theorem.

   bound the saturated/null LR statistic by 2m*KL(hat p_m || p).

   converges to a positive KL gap (or use Pinsker + fixed separation).

   the ACTUAL parameter-dependent tail functions. The nuisance mean

   Keep this distinct from an asymptotic fixed-threshold CDF result.
   Positive strict matching gaps guarantee eventual correct ML orientation.
